Questions office managers ask us

Is it the same crew every night?

Yes. You get an assigned crew and a named supervisor. If someone is out, the supervisor covers or sends a trained backup who has read your site notes.

What happens if something is missed?

Tell us by noon and we re-clean that area within 24 hours at no charge. Every miss is logged so it doesn’t happen twice.

How do you handle keys and alarm codes?

Keys are tagged with a code, never your address, and kept in a locked box. Alarm codes are stored encrypted and only the assigned crew can see them.

Do you clean during business hours?

Most sites are cleaned after close. Clinics and retail stores can add a daytime porter for restrooms and spills.

Is there a long contract?

Month to month after the first 90 days. Cancel with 30 days’ notice.
